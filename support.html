HOW TO PLAY

Frequently asked questions.

Q1 · SETUP

The keyboard doesn’t show up.

Go to Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard, and choose Dual Wield Keyboard. Then, in any text field, touch and hold the globe key and pick Dual Wield Keyboard from the list. The app’s setup guide walks through the same steps.

Q2 · FULL ACCESS

Should I turn on “Allow Full Access”?

No need. The keyboard never asks for Full Access, and every feature works without it.

Q3 · LEFT-HANDED

I want to type with my left hand.

Press ⇄ in the middle of the keyboard to swap the sides. The keyboard remembers your choice.

Q4 · CONVERSION

It doesn’t learn the words I use often.

That’s by design. It never stores what you type or the words you pick, so conversion doesn’t learn.

Q5 · WHAT YOU TYPE

Is what I type sent anywhere?

No. The keyboard never uses the network, and Japanese conversion runs on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Q6 · GLOBE KEY

There’s no globe key on the keyboard.

On devices that show a globe key below the keyboard, use that one. On those devices the keyboard doesn’t show its own.

Q7 · TRIAL AND PURCHASE

How do the trial and purchase work?

The app is free. Start a free 7-day trial in the app; to keep using it, buy it once in the app ($3.99). No subscription. Apple handles purchases and refunds; request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
